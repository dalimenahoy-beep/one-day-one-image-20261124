# One Day, One Image — Tuesday 24 November 2026

A single-image front page of the world's news for **Tuesday 24 November 2026**, drawn entirely in code: *The World Gazette* leads with Nvidia reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO after President Trump's intervention — shares up 31%, about $1 trillion added in a day — joined by Ukraine sinking the Russian corvette Merkury off Benghazi, COP31 ending without a deal, three tankers reported mined in the Strait of Hormuz, a Russian drone in Polish airspace, the Democrats' largest House majority in decades, the Banten candy-factory fire, strikes on buses in Yemen, 14 countries acting on the ICJ's Rohingya genocide ruling, a car bomb in Colombia, surging markets (Nikkei +3.91%), and Pakistan's first astronaut bound for China's Tiangong station — twelve stories across seven regions, plus a day-in-numbers box and a wire ticker.

The poster (`entry.png`, 1024×1024) is drawn at build time by `make_poster.py` (Python/PIL) and committed to the repo. The shipped program (`main.ts`, Node standard library only) does no drawing: it copies the committed `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`), so every run writes identical bytes. No network, no clocks, no randomness.
